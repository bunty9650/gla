FAQ

Answers before you apply.

How do I get admission to GLA Online MBA?

Submit the form, speak with an admission counselor, complete registration, upload documents, pay the applicable fee, and get access to the Learning Management System.

What is the eligibility for the program?

For MBA, applicants should hold a recognised bachelor's degree of minimum 3 years duration with at least 50% marks. Final eligibility is confirmed after document review.

Can I pay the fees through EMI?

The MBA fee is listed as Rs. 1,05,300, with a Rs. 500 registration charge and flexible payment options including one-time, annual, semester-wise, and EMI options.

Is placement support included?

Yes. GLA Online highlights placement support including career counseling, resume building, mock interviews, and access to recruiter opportunities.
